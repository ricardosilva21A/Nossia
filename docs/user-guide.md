# User guide

**English** · [Português](./guia-de-utilizacao.md)

The app is currently in Portuguese, so the names of screens and buttons appear here as they do in the app.

## Getting started

1. Create the account with your names and relationship date.
2. In **Definições** (avatar) (Settings, under the avatar at the top right), open **Perfil do casal** (Couple profile) and create the invite link for your partner.
3. On iPhone, open the site in Safari, tap **Share** and then **Add to Home Screen**.

## Adding things quickly

In the box on **Hoje** (Today), write it the way you'd say it: "Jantar italiano sexta às 20h". Nossia picks out the day and time. Choose whether it's a date, a commitment, an idea for later or something you've already done.

## Ideas

Browse the catalogue, save favourites, draw one at random, or answer a few questions to get suggestions that suit the weather and season.

## Restaurants and places

In **Explorar → Sítios** (Explore → Places), choose between trying somewhere new and going back to somewhere you liked. When you say you'd repeat a dinner, the restaurant moves to **Aprovados** (Approved), along with what you ate.

## Changing plans

Use the pencil on a card to change a date's day, time or place, or the × to delete it. Commitments, tasks and trips can be edited too.

## After a date

Tap **Já fizemos** (We did it). Nossia asks whether you'd repeat it and, depending on the date, what you watched or ate. You can add notes and photos.

## Films and series

On **Hoje**, **Vimos um filme ou série** logs it straight away and asks for the title. Repeat it as often as you like.

## Agenda

In **Agenda → Semana** (Week), add your work hours so Nossia can show when you're both free, and keep track of commitments and tasks.

## Trips

Plan destinations and what to do each day. After a trip, add photos and see it on **Mapa-mundo** (World map). Change your departure airport in **Definições** (avatar).

## Notifications and calendar

Turn on notifications in **Definições** (avatar). On iPhone they only work with Nossia on the Home Screen. In the same settings, you can subscribe to your dates in the iPhone calendar.

## Deleting your account

It's in **Definições** (avatar). If your partner has an account, the couple's data stays with them. If it's only you, everything is deleted, including photos.
