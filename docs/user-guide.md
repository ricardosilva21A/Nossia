# User guide

**English** · [Português](./guia-de-utilizacao.md)

The app is currently in Portuguese, so the names of screens and buttons appear here as they do in the app.

## Getting started

1. Create the account with your names and relationship date.
2. Create your avatar and tell it your birthday. The first one in also picks the house pet (for example, Summer).
3. In **Definições** (Settings, under the avatar at the top), open **Perfil do casal** (Couple profile) and create the invite link for your partner.
4. On iPhone, open the site in Safari, tap **Share** and then **Add to Home Screen**. Always open Nossia from that icon: it's the only way to get notifications.

## The house

**Hoje** (Today) is your house. Almost everything in it can be tapped:

- **You**: opens your profile.
- **Your partner**: send a kiss, a hug, a wave, "I miss you" or "thinking of you". They get a notification and can send one back.
- **The pet**: pick what it wears.
- **The window**: today's weather and the next days.
- **The wall map**: your trips, one pin per place, and **Marcar viagem** (plan a trip).
- **The calendar**: flip through the months, tap a day to see what's on and plan a date on it.

When one of you is at work or out, it shows as a short line above the sofa.


The house has rooms: **Hoje** is the living room, **Agenda** the kitchen, **Nós** the bedroom and **Explorar** the garden. Each of you is where you'd be at that hour: breakfast in the kitchen the hour before work (each at their own time; a lie-in until 8 without work), cooking, tidying or on the sofa while the other is at work, and asleep in the bedroom at night, with the living-room light off. The pet follows you around the house: it eats from its named bowl in the kitchen, lies on its bed in the bedroom and goes out through the dog flap to play in the fenced garden. It waits at the door when you're both out and sleeps in its bed when everyone is asleep. When you're both home you make dinner together, and some evenings whoever plays games sits at the bedroom computer while the other lies on the bed with the pet (and now and then joins in). Turn on **Jogo no computador** in **Definições › O meu avatar** to show up at the desk; playing alone, the pet lies at your feet. If someone got home while the app was closed, their arrival is replayed when you open it: they walk in and the pet runs to the door; a few minutes before someone leaves work, the pet is already waiting there. A kiss or hug you receive also shows in the living room, a date marked as done means a party with confetti, and on Friday and Saturday nights the pet sleeps at the foot of your bed. The garden changes with the weather and the season: flowers in spring, the pool in the sun, brown leaves in autumn, bare branches in winter, rain when it rains, and at Christmas snow, a snowman and a decorated tree. Whoever isn't in the living room shows up in a short line above the sofa.

**Birthdays and the anniversary:** in **Nós**, tap the anniversary or either birthday and choose when to celebrate: on the day, the day before, the weekend, the whole week (marked on the calendar) or another day. Then pick something to do or just mark the day. Three weeks ahead, if nothing is planned, the house reminds whoever should plan it (each birthday only shows to the other one).
## Adding things quickly

Under the house there are three buttons: **Date**, **Compromisso** (commitment) and **Ideia para depois** (idea for later). Type it the way you'd say it: "Jantar italiano sexta às 20h". Nossia understands the day and time.

## Proposals

A date with a day that one of you plans is a **proposal**: the other gets a notification and answers **Aceitar** (accept), **Outro dia** (another day) or **Agora não** (not now), in the house bubble or in **Agenda › Planeados**. Changing the day or time of a date asks the other one again. While you wait, you see "À espera de …" (waiting for …).

## Saving ideas from other apps

Saw a place on Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp or Maps? Copy the link and paste it into **Ideia para depois**. Nossia fetches its name, and you choose to keep it for later or plan a day. On iPhone you can also make a shortcut in the Shortcuts app that puts Nossia in the Share menu.

## Ideas

**Explorar** (Explore) is one screen. At the top, **Surpreendam-nos** (one idea at a time), **Escolher pelo feeling** and search. Below, short rows: **Novidades perto de vocês** (places the house found around home, once a week), **Guardadas por vocês** and **Desta época**, plus restaurants, cafés and bars. **Ver todas as ideias** shows the whole catalogue. Now and then the house asks whether you fancy each idea: right to like, left to pass. Your partner never sees what you pass.

**Couple games** live in Explorar too: **Quanto me conheces?** (answer about yourself, then guess your partner), **Isto ou aquilo** (this or that), **Quem é mais…?** (who is more likely to…), **Sonhos** (dreams) and **Memórias** (memories). Your partner's answers only show once you've answered too, with a reaction for a right guess and another for a wrong one. In the house, the bubble shows the question of the day and tells you when your partner answered. What each of you says about yourself is kept: it shows up as hints in **Prendas** (gifts), and the places you want to go show up on the wall map.

## Gifts

In **Nós › Prendas** (Us › Gifts) you keep gift ideas for your partner throughout the year: a name, a link and a note. Only you see your list; your partner never does. It shows how long until their birthday, your anniversary and Christmas, and the reminder a week before says how many ideas you have saved. A shared link can also be saved as a **Prenda**.

## Restaurants and places

In **Explorar**, under **Restaurantes, cafés e bares**, choose between trying somewhere new and going back to one you liked.

## After a date

An hour after the date ends, "Como correu?" (how did it go?) arrives. Three taps save it: 😍 Adorámos, 🙂 Foi bom or 😐 Mais ou menos, a photo if you want, done. **Escrever mais** (write more) opens the full memory (notes, place, what you watched or ate). **Já fizemos** (we did it) does the same.

## Agenda

**Agenda › Planeados** holds dates, proposals, commitments and the next days you are both free. Each person's work hours are changed in **Definições**.

## Trips

Plan destinations and what to do each day in **Agenda › Viagens**. After a trip, add photos and see it in **Nós › Mapa › Viagens** (Us › Map › Trips). The departure airport is set in **Definições**.

## Holidays and long weekends

In **Agenda › Viagens**, Nossia shows the next long weekends and bridge days worth taking, for example: "8 December is a Tuesday: take Monday off and you get 4 days in a row". It takes both work schedules into account, including weekend shifts, and your municipality's holiday. It also sends a notification three weeks before.

In **Definições**, under **As minhas férias** (my holidays), each person says how they take time off: **Quando quero** (whenever I want), with days per year and hard months, or **Marcadas pela empresa** (set by the company), with the periods.

## Notifications and calendar

In **Definições**, turn on notifications and choose what you want (dates, commitments, affection…). On iPhone they only work with Nossia on the Home Screen; Today reminds you until they're on. In the same settings you can subscribe to your dates in the iPhone calendar.

## Month review and weather

On the 1st you get a review of the month that ended (dates, 😍, trips), which also shows on Today during the first week and in **Nós › Números**. Weather notes only come when they change a plan: rain on an outdoor date, or a sunny weekend when you're both free, at most once a week. Turn them off in **Definições**, under **Tempo**.

## Deleting the account

It's in **Definições**. If your partner has an account, the couple's data stays with them. If it's just you, everything is deleted, including photos.
